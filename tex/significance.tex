\textbf{Significance statement: \\
Linear Kelvin Wave Predictions in the $z\to 0$ Limit}
\vspace{1cm}

\noindent
Dear Editor and Reviewers of JFM Rapids

\vspace{1cm}

Linear wave theory is a timeless cornerstone of fluid mechanics, but the linear potential for a translating disturbance near the free surface has long suffered from ill-posed behavior. This paper resolves this singularity, developing a regularized kernel with finite energy and consistent spectral decay, without the introduction of empirical damping. The analytic contribution clarifies fundamental wave generation mechanisms for finite-width geometries, providing insight into free-surface energy distribution and interference patterns. Coupled with a fast, uniformly accurate numerical method, the new theory enables practical use in high-speed design, control, and surrogate modeling, and establishes a robust linear foundation for studying free-surface fluid mechanics.
